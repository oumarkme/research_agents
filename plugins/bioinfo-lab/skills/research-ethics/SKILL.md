# Research ethics and compliance

* Humans: confirm the ethics approval and the consent scope cover the planned analysis and sharing before you start. Use the minimum data needed, de-identify, restrict access, and keep patient-level data off external services, including AI tools.
* Law differs by place (for example GDPR, HIPAA, and Taiwan's Human Biobank Management Act, Human Subjects Research Act, and Personal Data Protection Act). Name the rule that applies to the dataset and check it against the official text; secondary summaries are not enough.
* Results to participants: decide with the clinician whether and how incidental findings return. Use the current ACMG secondary-findings list, and check its version.
* Animals: approval from the institutional animal care committee, and the 3Rs (replace, reduce, refine).
* Germplasm: check material transfer agreements and benefit-sharing rules (ITPGRFA, Nagoya Protocol) before moving or publishing material.
* Authorship: ICMJE criteria (substantial contribution, drafting or revising, final approval, accountability). Agree on author order at project start.
* AI tools: they cannot be authors. Disclose use beyond copy editing in the Methods or acknowledgments, as ICMJE and Springer Nature require; check the target journal's current policy. Do not paste unpublished manuscripts under review, patient data, or confidential grants into external AI tools.
* If a step may breach consent, law, or policy, stop and ask the user. Do not decide it yourself.
