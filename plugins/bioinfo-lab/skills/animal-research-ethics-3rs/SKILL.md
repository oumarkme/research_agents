# Animal research ethics and the 3Rs

## Capability
Get ethics approval and permits before any work, design to replace, reduce, and refine, and report with the current animal-research reporting guideline. Law and committee rules differ by country and institution.

## Decide
1. Whether a non-animal method answers the question.
2. Species, strain, sex, and age, each justified by the question.
3. Sample size: effect size and its basis, variance source, alpha, power, unit of analysis (`study-design`); label a pilot guess.
4. Bias control (randomization, blinding, inclusion and exclusion rules), written before the work.
5. Humane endpoints, analgesia, housing, and monitoring.
6. Which approval covers the work, and which permits (collecting, handling, land access, export, import) apply for field and wildlife work.

## Find current information
Follow `current-methods-lookup`. Fetch and cite with retrieval date:
* What does your animal care committee require, and which forms and protocol version apply? (institution ethics office)
* Which national law and license levels apply? (national competent authority)
* Which permits does the field site or species need? (wildlife authority, import and export office)
* What is the current reporting guideline and checklist? (guideline home page, journal instructions)
* What does the 3Rs framework currently say? (see `references/where-to-look.md`)

## Verify
Open the approval letter and permits. Match approval number, protocol version, species, numbers, procedures, people, and dates to the planned work. An expired approval, a different procedure, or a permit that covers collection but not export means stop and ask the committee or permit authority. File an amendment before any protocol change.

## Failure modes
* Sample size set by habit.
* Pseudoreplication: animals, cages, or litters treated as independent.
* Exclusions and deaths not counted.
* Claiming the 3Rs or the guideline improved welfare: not verified.

## Record and report
Store approval number, protocol version, dates, amendments, permit IDs beside sample IDs, retrieval dates of rules, and the responsible person. Report these in Methods with the guideline checklist.
