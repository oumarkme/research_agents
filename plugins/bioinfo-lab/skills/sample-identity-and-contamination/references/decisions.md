# When a check fails

Cause first, then decision. Record each step in the project log.

| Finding | Likely cause | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Sex mismatch only | Sheet typo, or a real sex-chromosome variant | Ask the submitter (clinical team or collaborator) to confirm recorded sex. Hold the sample. Review X and Y coverage for aneuploidy. |
| Sex mismatch plus wrong relatedness | Swap | Hold the sample and its pair. Trace the plate map and the extraction batch. Do not report. |
| Two unrelated samples with near-identical genotypes | Duplicate submission or swap | Compare with the array or earlier sample. Escalate. |
| Parent-child pair shows many Mendelian errors | Wrong parent, or low-quality sample | Recheck depth and contamination, then the pedigree. |
| Pedigree shows an unexpected relationship | Undisclosed relatedness or misattributed parentage | Report to the clinical lead. Do not edit the pedigree yourself. |
| FREEMIX in the review band | Index hopping, plate neighbor, or low depth | Check depth and run-level hopping. Find the likely source by the genotype mix. |
| FREEMIX in the fail band | Real contamination | Re-extract and re-sequence if the sample allows. Otherwise report with a stated limit. |
| Fingerprint disagrees with earlier sample | Swap, sample mix-up, or wrong earlier record | Test both records. Escalate. |

## Rules

* One signal is a flag. Two independent signals support a swap.
* Never relabel a sample to fit the data. Correct the sheet only after the wet lab and clinical lead agree, and log who and why.
* Contamination below the review band still goes in the report.
* Re-sequence only when a new library or a new extraction is the fix. Deeper sequencing of the same contaminated library does not help.
