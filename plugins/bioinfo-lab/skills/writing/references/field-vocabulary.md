# Field vocabulary

* Use the exact terms, spellings, and abbreviations that recent papers in the specific sub-field use (genomics, human genetics, plant breeding, quantitative genetics, genomic prediction, long-read sequencing). Do not paraphrase a technical term to avoid repeating it; repeat it.
* Check uncertain terms before use: search PubMed or recent reviews and prefer the form that appears most in the last five years of reputable journals. Prefer official nomenclature: HGNC and MGI gene symbols, HGVS variant notation (e.g., c.123A>G, p.Arg41Gly), Gene Ontology and Sequence Ontology terms, Ensembl or RefSeq IDs, standard crop gene and trait ontology names, SI units.
* Typical field wording to prefer (verify against the target paper's sub-field): *variant calling*, *read depth* or *coverage*, *allele frequency*, *linkage disequilibrium*, *heritability* (narrow-sense *h²*, broad-sense *H²*), *genomic estimated breeding value (GEBV)*, *prediction accuracy*, *cross-validation*, *marker-assisted selection*, *genome-wide association study (GWAS)*, *long-read sequencing*, *basecalling*, *haplotype-resolved assembly*, *structural variant*. Avoid generic stand-ins (*genetic changes*, *DNA reading*, *accuracy score*).
* In Chinese text keep the term itself in English (see `translator`). Keep a short glossary of project terms in the project README when a term has several accepted forms, and use one form only.
* If the target journal or lab already uses a form (e.g., "GBLUP", "ssGBLUP", "SNP array"), copy it exactly.
