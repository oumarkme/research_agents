## Variant filtering

Variants were annotated against GRCh38 with Ensembl VEP 112 (MANE Select NM_000059.4). We kept calls with GQ >= 30 and DP >= 10, and flagged BRCA2 c.5946del (p.Ser1982Argfs*22) and c.7007G>A (rs80358981). Allele frequencies came from gnomAD v4.1 (https://gnomad.broadinstitute.org); 3 of 41 candidates had AF > 0.001.
