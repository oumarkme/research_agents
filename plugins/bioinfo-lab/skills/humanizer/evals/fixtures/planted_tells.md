We delve into the intricate landscape of rare-variant calling, showcasing a robust pipeline that leverages minimap2 2.28 and DeepVariant 1.6.1. It is important to note that the pipeline is not only fast but also accurate, highlighting its pivotal role in the era of long-read sequencing. The 14 trios (NA12878 and 13 others) reached a mean coverage of 32.4× (SD 3.1), and recall for SNVs was 99.2% (95% CI 98.9-99.4; P = 0.003). Moreover, the variant NM_000546.6:c.743G>A (p.Arg248Gln; rs11540652) was called in 12 of 14 trios (Smith et al., 2024; PMID: 38000001). Furthermore, these results underscore the importance of coverage, speed, and cost, paving the way for clinical use.
